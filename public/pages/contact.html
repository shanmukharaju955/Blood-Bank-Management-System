Frequently Asked Questions

Clinical answers to common questions about donation safety, intervals, and emergency eligibility.

How often can I safely donate blood?

For standard whole blood donation (450ml), donors must wait at least 90 days (12 weeks) between donations to allow complete red blood cell hemoglobin regeneration and iron stores recovery. Apheresis platelet donors may donate every 14 days.

Is blood donation completely safe and sterile?

Yes, 100%. All needle sets, blood collection bags, and tubing are factory sealed, gamma-sterilized, and strictly single-use. They are unsealed immediately before your donation and safely bio-incinerated immediately afterwards. There is zero risk of contracting any bloodborne infection.

What should I do before coming in to donate?

Drink plenty of water (at least 16 oz), consume an iron-rich, healthy meal 2-3 hours beforehand, avoid fatty foods, and bring a government photo ID. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours prior.

How does BloodBank360 prevent expired blood distribution?

Our Node.js + MySQL backend performs strict database query constraints and transactional locks that categorically exclude batches where expiry_date < CURDATE(). Expired units are systematically flagged for autologous plasma reprocessing or research disposal.
