Lifecycle Reference Guide

Pending Requisition recorded in MySQL, queued for hematologist clinical review.
Approved Sufficient non-expired units verified and deducted from cold vaults.
Fulfilled Units dispatched in certified mobile cold-boxes and delivered to patient.
Rejected Requisition cannot be filled due to medical incompatibility or cancellation.
