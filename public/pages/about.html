Medical Excellence & Compassion

Pioneering Safe, Real-Time
Blood Transfusion Logistics

Founded by leading hematologists and emergency trauma surgeons, BloodBank360 operates as an advanced, certified non-profit blood banking authority serving over 120 partner hospitals and medical institutes.

Our Mission

To eradicate preventable fatalities stemming from blood shortages by mobilizing voluntary community donations, upholding rigorous immunohematological safety testing, and delivering rapid cold-chain fulfillment to emergency rooms within minutes.

Our Vision

A world where no patient—regardless of geographic location or rare blood phenotype—ever loses the fight for life due to an inaccessible transfusion unit. We aim to set global benchmarks in digital blood logistics transparency.

Advanced Cold-Chain

Precision Temperature Preservation & Nucleic Acid Screening

Whole blood, packed red cells, fresh frozen plasma, and platelets each require distinct biological micro-environments. Our automated refrigeration systems keep whole blood strictly between 2°C and 6°C, while cryo-vaults store plasma at -30°C.

  • Individual Nucleic Acid Testing (NAT) for zero-window pathogen detection
  • Continuous IoT thermal sensors with automated backup power grids
  • RFID-tagged smart blood bags with barcode lot-traceability
